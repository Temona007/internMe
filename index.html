    About

    InternMe is a small marketplace for people early in their career and people building the first version of something.

    Reels stay at 30 seconds so founders can actually watch them. Projects stay structured so a junior knows the length, the place, and the work. Zip and area filters keep on-site roles honest. Remote listings say remote, and they do not pretend to have a commute.

    The featured grids never show more than six cards a side. Curation is the product. If a filter matches more than six, only the first six render.
